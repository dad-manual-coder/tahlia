 WHERE TO DOWNLOAD FREE, HIGH-QUALITY IMAGES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  General sports (free):
  • https://unsplash.com  — search "football", "soccer stadium", "crowd"
  • https://commons.wikimedia.org — search player/match names
  • https://www.pexels.com — similar to Unsplash

  Chelsea-specific (check individual image licenses):
  • Google Images → Tools → Size: Large → filter by usage rights
  • Chelsea FC official site (chelseasfc.com) — press section
  • Getty Images free tier (for editorial/personal use)

  NOTE: For a personal fan project, editorial use of press photos
  is generally acceptable. Always credit the photographer where possible.

 IMAGE SIZING GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  hero_stadium.jpg            → min 1920×1080px  (16:9 landscape)
  player_*.jpg                → min 400×500px    (portrait, face at top)
  drogba_main.jpg             → min 600×800px    (portrait, face upper third)
  gallery_stamford_bridge.jpg → min 800×500px    (landscape preferred)
  gallery_*.jpg               → min 600×400px    (landscape preferred)

  All images use object-fit:cover so exact dimensions don't matter —
  just ensure the key subject (face, badge, action) is centred or at top.
